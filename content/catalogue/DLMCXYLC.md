description_en: This is an in-depth anthology of fiction with gay themes by twenty-four
  writers, among them the internationally acclaimed Manuel Puig (Argentina), Mário
  de Andrade (Brazil), and Reinaldo Arenas (Cuba). The book presents Latin American
  gays as part of a lively, fascinating social reality—as soldiers, businessmen, office
  workers, students, cattle ranchers, circus performers, and more. Included are two
  complete novellas—one about homosexuality in the marines, the other about a sexual
  encounter between two high school boys—as well as the brilliant "Orgy," an erotic
  diary based on experiences in tropical Brazil. Nearly four hundred pages contain
  thirty different selections by a dazzling array of talented writers.
