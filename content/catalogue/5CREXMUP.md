description_en: Set in Cuba during the 1920s, this novel recounts the eventful life
  of a cabaret dancer named Rachel. The protagonist is an actress, a dancer, and a
  heartbreaker who takes readers on a guided tour through the different stages of
  her past, from the dingy theaters, cabarets, and circuses in which she debuted to
  the mythical Alhambra Theater in which she reached the height of her career. In
  addition to Rachel's narrative, the voices of stagehands, journalists, friends,
  enemies, and gossips depict an accurate portrait of the customs of the Cuban belle
  époque.
description_es: Ambientada en Cuba durante los años veinte, esta novela relata la

  debutó hasta el mítico Teatro Alhambra, donde alcanzó el apogeo de su carrera. Además
