description_en: Controversial and compelling, *In Cold Blood* reconstructs the 1959
  murder of a Kansas farmer, his wife, and both their children. Truman Capote's comprehensive

  this terrible crime and its effect on those involved. At the centre of his study
  are the amoral young killers Perry Smith and Dick Hickock, who, vividly drawn by
  Capote, are shown to be reprehensible yet entirely and frighteningly human. The
  book that made Capote's name, *In Cold Blood* is a seminal work of modern prose
  and a remarkable synthesis of journalistic skill and powerfully evocative narrative.
